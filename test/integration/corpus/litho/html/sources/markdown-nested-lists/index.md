# Nested lists

1. The declaration has a preamble and thirty articles.
   - The preamble gives the reasons:
     1. recognition of inherent dignity;
     2. disregard for human rights led to barbarous acts;
     3. the rule of law protects human rights.
   - The articles set out the rights themselves.

2. Each article can be cited by number.

   A loose item holds paragraphs, and a code block:

   ```
   article 1: free and equal in dignity and rights
   article 3: life, liberty and security of person
   ```

   > A block quote inside an item: "All human beings are born free and equal in dignity and rights."

3. Third item, with a list four levels deep:
   - first level
     - second level
       - third level
         - fourth level

- An unordered list after the ordered one, with `inline code` and **strong** words.
- A second bullet
    - indented by four spaces
