Quarterly field report

Measurements from the three northern stations, with notes beside the terms they explain.

The stations log the dew point every ten minutes and send a daily summary. Over the quarter the readings stayed within the usual band, with one cold spell in the second month that pulled the averages down for a week.

Each summary also carries the wind run, the distance the air travelled past the mast in a day. It rose steadily through the quarter as the season turned, and the eastern station, on the ridge, logged the largest figures by a wide margin.

Rain gauges were checked by hand twice in the quarter. The second check found the western gauge partly blocked by leaves, so its readings for the last three weeks are marked as provisional until the station is serviced.

Dew point: the temperature at which the air would be saturated; it tracks moisture, not warmth.
Wind run: the daily sum of wind speed over time, in kilometres.
Provisional: kept in the totals but flagged until a second reading confirms it.

Station layout

The second figure shows the ridge station's layout: the mast at the centre, the gauges in a row along the fence, and the shelter at the far end. The badge above the figure and the caption below it are placed from the figure itself.

The fence line was moved in the spring, which shifted the gauges by a metre; the offset is applied to every reading taken after the move.

Offset: a fixed correction of 0.4 mm a day, from the side-by-side test.
