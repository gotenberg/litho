From the board

The season opened with a full hall and a long queue at the door. We thank the volunteers who kept the evening running and the speakers who stayed late to answer every question.

Next month we move to the old library on the square. Members keep their seats, and the reading room stays open on Thursdays.

Renewals are due by the end of the month. The fee is unchanged.

Programme

Three talks, a walk through the archive and the annual dinner. The full list is on the back page.
