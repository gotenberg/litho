Introduction

The survey covered the north shore over four weeks of fieldwork, from the harbour wall to the dunes.

Method

Each transect was walked at low tide, and every find was logged with its position and depth.

Results

The upper beach held most of the finds; the lower beach held fewer, and the dunes fewer still.

Discussion

The pattern follows the winter storms, which move material up the beach and leave it at the strand line.
