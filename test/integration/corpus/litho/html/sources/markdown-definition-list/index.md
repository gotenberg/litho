# Definitions

Declaration
: A formal statement adopted by the General Assembly of the United Nations on 10 December 1948.

Article
: One of the thirty numbered provisions of the declaration.
: The unit by which the rights are cited.

Preamble
: The seven recitals that come before the articles.

    > Whereas recognition of the inherent dignity and of the equal and inalienable rights of all members of the human family is the foundation of freedom, justice and peace in the world.

Right
: A freedom or entitlement the declaration sets out, from life and liberty (article 3) to education (article 26).
