# litho/odp corpus fixtures

OpenDocument presentations that lock how a conversion applies the paper, the orientation and the notes pages to a deck, scored by the `diff` feature (see `FEATURES.md`) against LibreOffice Impress. No corpus of LibreOffice's test documents carries the route's options.

Each source is a directory, `sources/<name>/`, holding one flat OpenDocument file, plain XML that lives in git, and optionally `options.json`, the LibreOffice route's form fields it converts with (`fixture.LoadOffice` reads them as for the other Office corpora: `paperWidth` and `paperHeight` in inches, `landscape`, `nativePages`, `nativeBookmarks`, `exportNotesPages`). A source that differs from another by its options alone links that source's document. The references are LibreOffice's, built by `corpus references` with the image `GOTENBERG_IMAGE` in `corpus/versions.env` pins (`gotenberg/gotenberg:9.0.0`, LibreOffice 26.8.2.2) through `/forms/libreoffice/convert`, into the testdata, never committed. The documents draw Liberation Serif and Liberation Sans, which the image and the fixture fonts both carry, and were written by a script for this corpus.

Impress prints no handout from the command line or through its PDF export, so no reference engine prints `handouts-deck`'s handouts. Each `handouts-N` option fixture names `handouts-N-ref` as its `reference_of`: a document whose plain conversion is the expected handout pages, whose LibreOffice reference the `diff` cell scores litho's handouts against. Each page of a `-ref` document takes the name of its first slide, so the bookmark LibreOffice writes for it is one litho writes, leading to the same page. The `diff` cell so scores how litho draws a handout page, not where it places the slides: the `-ref` documents take their rectangles from litho's rule. That rule is locked to LibreOffice by `TestHandoutAreas_Impress` in `pkg/internal/convert/slides`: the 48 layouts the pinned LibreOffice stores in the handout master, for every count of slides a page.

## Accepted disagreements

Impress applies neither the route's paper nor its landscape to a presentation: it prints each slide on a page of the slide size. litho follows its own rule, the same in every format: the caller's paper is every page, and a slide is scaled down to fit it, never up, and centred. `wide-deck-paper` and `portrait-deck-landscape` cannot match the reference's page sizes, so they fail the `diff` cell by design: their `pages`, `layout` and `strict` scores are 0, which a change can neither raise nor lower, and their two mismatches in the cell's count mean only that. The paper rule they exercise is locked by the public API tests instead (`TestConvert_SlidesFitThePaper` in `pkg/pdf/convert_pagesetup_test.go`, which scales a wide slide to Letter and turns a portrait one).

They stay in the scored set rather than in the manifest's `skips`, which would drop them from every feature: their `text_f1`, `link_recall` and `outline_recall` still read 1 against LibreOffice and gate the text a scaled or turned slide keeps, and the `targets` cell converts them at their options to every output flavour, the only fixtures of the matrix that hold a presentation's paper and orientation options to the PDF/A and PDF/UA profiles.

## Paper documents

`corpus build` generates one deck per slide size from the committed `paper-deck` into the testdata (`paperDocs` in `cmd/corpus/papers.go`, `papers/<name>/`), never committed. `paper-deck` is the deck of `wide-deck` on Screen 4:3, 28 by 21 cm; each generated deck changes the slide page layout alone.
