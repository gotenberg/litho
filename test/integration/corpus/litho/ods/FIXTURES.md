# litho/ods corpus fixtures

OpenDocument spreadsheets that lock how a conversion applies the paper and the orientation to the sheets of a workbook, scored by the `diff` feature (see `FEATURES.md`) against LibreOffice Calc. No corpus of LibreOffice's test documents carries the route's options.

Each source is a directory, `sources/<name>/`, holding one flat OpenDocument file, plain XML that lives in git, and optionally `options.json`, the LibreOffice route's form fields it converts with (`fixture.LoadOffice` reads them as for the other Office corpora: `paperWidth` and `paperHeight` in inches, `landscape`, `nativePages`, `nativeBookmarks`, `exportNotesPages`). A source that differs from another by its options alone links that source's document. The references are LibreOffice's, built by `corpus references` with the image `GOTENBERG_IMAGE` in `corpus/versions.env` pins (`gotenberg/gotenberg:9.0.0`, LibreOffice 26.8.2.2) through `/forms/libreoffice/convert`, into the testdata, never committed. The documents draw Liberation Serif and Liberation Sans, which the image and the fixture fonts both carry, and were written by a script for this corpus; the sheet selection documents are LibreOffice's flat saves of `litho/xlsx`'s `sheet_selection` and its twins, in Calibri, which Carlito stands in for. The options `sheets`, `sheetNumbers` and `hiddenSheets` are litho's own: no route takes them, so their sources borrow, by `reference_of`, the reference of a document that holds exactly the sheets they print.

LibreOffice prints a page that names no size on Letter, the paper of its en-US locale; litho takes `pdf.WithDefaultPaper`, A4 without it, so the `diff` feature converts these sources with a Letter default paper (`no-page-size` scores the size otherwise).

## Paper documents

`corpus build` generates the paper documents from the committed `paper-grid` and `paper-fit` into the testdata (`paperDocs` in `cmd/corpus/papers.go`, `papers/<name>/`), never committed. They are the workbooks of `litho/xlsx`'s bases in ODF: a grid of 20 columns by 130 rows with a header and a footer, and 16 columns by 120 rows fitted to one page wide (`style:scale-to-X` 1, `style:scale-to-Y` 0), on portrait Letter. The LibreOffice route takes the paper options, so the option documents have references of their own. Measured on the references: LibreOffice prints each option workbook as it prints the workbook stating that paper itself, the same pages and the same text (`paper-grid-option-a5-portrait`: 20 pages, as `paper-grid-a5-portrait`).

| Fixture                                                                             | Count | Page setup                                                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `paper-grid`, `paper-fit`                                                           | 2     | Committed, portrait Letter                                                                                 |
| `paper-grid-<paper>-<orientation>`                                                  | 16    | The page layout on A3, A4, A5 and B5 (ISO 216), Letter, Legal, Tabloid and Executive, in both orientations |
| `paper-fit-<paper>-<orientation>`                                                   | 6     | The fitted sheet on A5, Letter and A3, in both orientations                                                |
| `paper-grid-option-<paper>-<orientation>`, `paper-fit-option-<paper>-<orientation>` | 12    | The base with A5, Letter or A3 as options, and `landscape` for the landscape ones                          |
